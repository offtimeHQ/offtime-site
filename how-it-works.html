Security & privacy

Your Mac stays yours.

People don’t get remote access to your Mac. Offtime runs supported workloads separately from your personal environment.

What renters can use

  • Offtime compute
  • Approved workload environment
  • Allocated resources

What renters cannot use

  • Your files
  • Your desktop
  • Your browser
  • Your apps
  • Your Apple account
  • Your personal data

Offtime limits renters to supported workload environments. As with any software running on hardware, security depends on the safeguards and workload isolation Offtime implements; it is not a promise of absolute protection from every possible threat.
