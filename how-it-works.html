How Offtime works

Compute is everywhere.
Most of it is idle.

Every day, millions of computers sit unused for hours. At the same time, builders are paying for compute they need to get work done.

Offtime connects the two.

01

The gap is access.

If you need compute today, you usually rent it from a cloud provider.

If you own a powerful machine, most of its capacity goes unused when you're away.

These two sides rarely meet.

We think they should.

02

Offtime is a network for compute.

People with spare compute can make it available.

People who need compute can use it.

Offtime handles the connection between them.

Have compute→Share it→Earn

Need compute→Submit work→Get results

03

Your computer doesn't have to sit idle.

Your Mac is probably doing nothing while you're sleeping, working, or away.

Offtime lets you share that spare capacity and earn from workloads running on it.

Your Mac. Your rules.

Choose when your Mac is available.
Pause anytime.

MacBook · Mac mini · Mac Studio

04

Need compute?
Don't worry about the machine.

Submit your workload to Offtime.

We find available compute, run the work, and return the result.

Your workload ↓ Offtime ↓ Available compute ↓ Your result

You give us the work.
We figure out the compute.

05

Behind the scenes,
Offtime does the hard part.

  1. 01

    Match

    We find compute that can handle your workload.

  2. 02

    Route

    Your work is sent to an available machine.

  3. 03

    Run

    The workload runs while that compute is available.

  4. 04

    Return

    Your result comes back to you.

If something goes wrong, Offtime handles retries and keeps the workflow moving.

06

You're not renting a machine.

You don't browse servers.
You don't pick a CPU.
You don't manage infrastructure.

You rent compute.

Tell Offtime what you need done.
We'll figure out where it should run.

07

One network.
Many machines.

A MacBook at night.

A Mac mini sitting unused.

A workstation after hours.

Compute that would otherwise sit idle can become part of the network.

08

We're starting with
the Macs you already own.

MacBooks.
Mac minis.
Mac Studios.

Apple Silicon is our first network.

We're building the infrastructure to make distributed compute feel simple, regardless of what machine is underneath.

09

Your computer.
Your control.

Offtime is built around spare capacity, not unrestricted access to your computer.

You decide when your machine is available.
You can pause sharing whenever you want.

Your personal files aren't automatically accessible to Offtime.
